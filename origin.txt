humanity last forum

This is a project that intend to preserve online forums that is fully human. 

With the advent of digital AI, forums like Reddit & online communities will be mainly bots
(see Dead Internet Theory). This is a project aiming to preserve communities online that is
fully human, working with the public on identifying what content is 'digital AI' & creating new
technologies to help preserve humanity's freedom of speech. 

I think the most interesting point here is "a seperation between where/when AI is allowed and AI is not allowed".
It is very important to have a place that is not impacted by AI because we need the raw grounded truth. Yet, I 
am a firm believer that AI is a fundemantal technology that does help with information processing. I am creating
this project because I believe in what I just said. I think most of the internet will be integrated with AI but
having a space that is completely grounded in human truth matters a lot for humanity's future.
